## About section

Maison Tanneurs is a leather house working from Marrakech.

We make full-grain leather bags in small editions: crossbodies, satchels, totes, backpacks, briefcases, and travel pieces cut and stitched with the patience of the bench.

The work is direct: leather, thread, brass, hand, repair. No wholesale margin, no anonymous stock, no decorative story added after the fact. Each object is made to carry well, mark honestly, and return to the atelier when it needs care.

## Short story / production process

Each Maison Tanneurs piece begins at the bench: hides are selected, panels are cut, edges are worked, hardware is set, and stitching is finished by hand. Some editions use handwoven textile panels framed in leather; others are pure full-grain leather silhouettes built for daily use and travel.

We ship directly from the atelier and support every piece after purchase through repair.

## Shipping policy

We offer free worldwide shipping on Maison Tanneurs pieces.

Orders usually leave the atelier within 3–5 business days when the item is in stock. You will receive tracking once the parcel is dispatched.

International orders may be subject to customs duties, VAT, or import taxes charged by the destination country. These charges are not collected by Maison Tanneurs and remain the buyer’s responsibility.

## Returns policy

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery.

To be eligible, the item must be unused, in its original condition, and returned with its original packaging. Please contact us before sending a return so we can confirm the correct return address and instructions.

Original shipping is complimentary; return shipping is the buyer’s responsibility unless the item arrives damaged or incorrect.

## Repairs policy

Maison Tanneurs offers lifetime repair support for its leather goods.

If stitching, hardware, edge work, or other repairable elements need attention, contact us with photos and your order details. We will assess the piece and propose the cleanest repair path.

Repair support does not cover misuse, severe accidental damage, or normal cosmetic patina, but we will always try to help preserve the object when repair is possible.
